00 — Position

Marn sells an outcome you can feel — less pain, more range — so the system is built on warmth and evidence, never on diagnosis.

Taken from your reference

Dark-first UI, soft-cornered cards, the ring as the hero metric, chip filters. It reads premium at a glance on a phone in a studio.

Deliberately left behind

Neon lime and electric cyan, gym photography, calorie language. Those cues say fitness performance; Marn is recovery.

Guardrails

No pure white, no pure black, no sharp corners. No cross, pulse-line, spine or anatomy iconography anywhere in the system.

01 — Logo

Four directions, each a single idea drawn in one weight. None stacks repeating strokes — that is what read as a signal meter — and none is a small circle with one stroke attached to it.

01Hinge
Two arms from one point of contact, held open. The most literal reading of range, and it works down to 16 px.
02Arcade
A lowercase m built as two arches — a nod to the studio architecture. The most wordmark-native option, and the only one that carries the name without a gap.
03Aperture
Two arcs bowing away from each other: the distance between where you were and where you are now.
04Orbit
A slim ellipse opening into a wider one. Reads as expansion, and the crossings give it a cut-gem quality.

Range-of-motion bands

Four states on a journey, not a triage. The ramp runs stone → ochre → jade → celadon: quiet neutral warming into colour, then cooling into the accent. Green sits in the middle, not at the top, and nothing in the set is red — so no band can be read as an alarm or an all-clear.

Ambient wash

Every screen carries one soft leak of colour from the top edge, fading out by roughly a quarter of the height. It is atmosphere, not information: nothing sits on it that needs to be read against it, and everything below it stays on flat obsidian so the data keeps full contrast. The hue is tied to the tab, so moving across the bar cross-fades the room rather than repainting the UI. Transition background 420 ms ease.

Selection is never carried by colour alone. Level 2 adds a long shadow and is reserved for things that genuinely float.
